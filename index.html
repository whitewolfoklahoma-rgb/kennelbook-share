How KennelBook works

Nights: a boarding stay is billed by nights — check-out date minus check-in date (in Friday, out Monday = 3 nights). A same-day in-and-out counts as 1 night. Daycare is billed per day, counting both the first and last day.

Vaccinations: rabies, bordetella, and distemper/parvo expiry dates live on each dog's record. A dog whose rabies is expired (or has no rabies date on file) is flagged CANNOT BOARD everywhere it matters — the dog list, the dashboard, and the booking form. Expired bordetella/distemper shows a warning, and any vaccine inside your "expiring soon" window (default 30 days) is flagged before it lapses.

Packages: when you sell a package (say 10 nights for $350), bookings you link to it draw nights off the balance. At pickup, the settlement statement credits those nights at the booking's rate — the owner only owes nights beyond the package, plus extras, minus anything already paid.

Settlement: every booking's statement shows nights/days × rate, extras, package credit, payments, and the balance due — printable for the owner's copy at pickup.

Honest limits: KennelBook is the kennel's own book. It does not take online bookings or card payments, send reminders, or sync between devices — export/import moves the book manually. It keeps records; it gives no veterinary or legal advice.
